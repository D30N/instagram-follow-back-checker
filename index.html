    How to get your Instagram ZIP

    It takes about 2 minutes to request. Instagram usually emails the download link within a few minutes to a few hours.

    1. Open Instagram, go to your profileTap the menu (three lines) at the top right, then Settings.
    2. Open Accounts CentreTap Accounts Centre, then Your information and permissions.
    3. Request your dataTap Download your information, then Download or transfer information and pick your account.
    4. Pick JSON formatChoose Some of your information, tick only Followers and following, then Next. Set Date range to All time and Format to JSON, then tap Create files.
    5. Download and upload hereOpen the link Instagram emails you, download the ZIP, then drop that ZIP in the box above.
    Why JSON, and what is inside the ZIP?

    Instagram also offers an HTML download, but this checker reads the JSON version, so please pick JSON when you request it. Inside the ZIP, your lists live in connections/followers_and_following/ as followers_1.json and following.json. If you have a very large account, followers may be split into followers_1.json, followers_2.json and so on. This page reads every part.

    Your password is never asked for here, and your ZIP never leaves your device. If a site asks for your Instagram login to show this list, do not use it.
